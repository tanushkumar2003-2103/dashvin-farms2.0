OUR HERITAGE

IT STARTS HERE.

For more than 10 years, Dashvin Farms has remained deeply rooted in authentic Indian dairy traditions. We believe pure dairy cannot be manufactured in a factory—it can only be nurtured with patience, compassion, and respect for nature.

Every morning at 4:30 AM, our cows are tended with natural green fodder grown on our own chemical-free fields. Untouched by synthetic hormones or commercial processing, what reaches your dining table is dairy just as nature intended: honest, wholesome, and profoundly nourishing.

THE DASHVIN SANCTUARY

WHERE PURITY BEGINS

A harmonious ecosystem where traditional Indian animal care meets modern hygienic standards.

RESPONSIBLE CARE

Healthy indigenous Gir cows and Murrah buffaloes raised with ethical affection. Free-grazing on open pastures, clean natural spring water, and veterinary care.

CLEAN FARM

Spotlessly sanitized open-air sheds with continuous ventilation, dry cobblestone flooring, and eco-friendly waste recycling into bio-gas and organic fertilizer.

HYGIENIC HANDLING

Strict untouched-by-hand protocol from milking clusters to chilling at 4°C within 15 minutes. Daily laboratory testing for purity, fat content, and zero adulteration.

FRESHNESS

Direct from farm to your home within hours of morning milking. Zero storage in intermediate warehouses, zero milk skimming, and natural cream-top integrity.

Ethical Pastoral Grazing

Our cattle roam freely in open lush pastures, nourished by native grasses, medicinal herbs, and natural sunlight.
