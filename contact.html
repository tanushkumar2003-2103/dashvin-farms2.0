FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about our milk, cattle welfare, and morning delivery.

Commercial packet milk comes from hybridized Western cows (HF/Jersey) containing the A1 beta-casein gene, which releases BCM-7 during digestion, often causing bloating, inflammation, and discomfort. Our indigenous Gir cows produce 100% natural A2 beta-casein, which is identical to the human digestive profile, easy on the stomach, and rich in natural nutrients.
We rely strictly on the unbroken cold chain. Within 15 minutes of 4:30 AM milking, our milk is rapidly chilled to 4°C in food-grade stainless steel chillers and bottled in sterilized glass bottles. It is transported in insulated vehicles and delivered to your doorstep by 7:00 AM. No preservatives, boiling powder, or synthetic stabilizers are ever added.
Every morning, simply rinse your empty glass bottle and place it in the insulated doorstep bag provided by Dashvin Farms. Our morning delivery partner will replace it with a fresh, sealed, cold milk bottle. The returned bottles are subjected to a rigorous 4-step medical-grade steam sterilization at the farm before reuse.
